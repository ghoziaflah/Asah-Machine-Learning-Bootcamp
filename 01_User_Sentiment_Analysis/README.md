# User Sentiment Analysis of Access by KAI Mobile Application
# 1. Business Understanding

Customer feedback plays an important role in understanding how users perceive a company's products and services. For digital platforms such as Access by KAI, customer reviews provide valuable insights into user satisfaction, service quality, and potential friction points throughout the customer journey.

However, with thousands of reviews generated by users, manually identifying recurring complaints and understanding overall customer perceptions can become inefficient and challenging. Without a structured approach to analyzing feedback, businesses may struggle to recognize common customer pain points and determine which areas require improvement.

This project aims to analyze customer feedback from the Access by KAI application to understand overall user sentiment, identify patterns in customer perceptions, and uncover potential areas for service improvement.

The analysis focuses on answering three key business questions:

- **How do customers perceive the application?** Understand the overall distribution of positive, negative, and neutral feedback.
- **What does customer feedback reveal about user experience?** Identify recurring concerns and patterns that may indicate friction points in the application.
- **How can customer feedback support business decisions?** Translate customer perceptions into insights that can help prioritize improvements in application usability and service quality.

# 2. Result Summary of the Project

The analysis covered **55,000 customer reviews** from the Access by KAI application on Google Play Store, providing a broad overview of customer perceptions and experiences.

### Customer Sentiment Distribution

| Sentiment | Number of Reviews | Percentage |
|---|---:|---:|
| Negative | 37,452 | 68.1% |
| Positive | 11,543 | 21.0% |
| Neutral | 6,005 | 10.9% |
| **Total** | **55,000** | **100%** |

The findings indicate that negative sentiment dominates customer feedback, accounting for approximately 68.1% of all collected reviews.

This suggests that a considerable proportion of users expressed dissatisfaction with their experience when interacting with the application.

Beyond understanding overall sentiment, the project also developed an automated approach to classify customer feedback, achieving a best testing accuracy of **95.02%**. This provides a foundation for processing customer reviews more efficiently and supporting continuous feedback monitoring.

# 3. Key Takeaways

- **Negative customer sentiment represents a significant concern.** With approximately 68.1% of reviews classified as negative, customer dissatisfaction deserves further investigation to understand the underlying causes.

- **Customer reviews provide valuable signals for service improvement.** Feedback can help businesses recognize potential issues across different aspects of the user experience, including application usability, booking processes, payment transactions, and technical reliability.

- **Customer feedback analysis can support better prioritization.** Rather than treating individual complaints separately, businesses can use aggregated feedback patterns to identify recurring issues and focus improvement efforts on areas affecting more users.

- **Automating feedback analysis creates opportunities for continuous monitoring.** Processing customer reviews at scale can help businesses track customer perceptions more efficiently and identify potential service issues without relying entirely on manual review.

# 4. Process

- Importing required Python libraries and loading 55,000 customer reviews from the Access by KAI application.
- Performing data understanding and inspection to examine dataset structure, completeness, and customer review characteristics.
- Conducting text preprocessing, including text cleaning, case folding, slang normalization, tokenization, stopword removal, and stemming.
- Performing sentiment classification and Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) to examine sentiment distribution, polarity, and frequently occurring words.
- Developing and comparing SVC, LSTM, and GRU models to automate customer sentiment classification.
- Evaluating model performance using accuracy, precision, recall, and F1-score, followed by testing sentiment predictions on new customer reviews.

# 5. Solution Statement

This project provides a structured approach to understanding customer perceptions through large-scale feedback analysis.

By transforming unstructured customer reviews into measurable sentiment categories, businesses can gain a clearer overview of user satisfaction and identify potential areas that require further investigation.

The findings highlight the importance of systematically monitoring customer feedback, particularly given the high proportion of negative sentiment observed in the dataset.

The proposed solution can serve as a foundation for a customer feedback monitoring system that enables businesses to:

- Monitor customer sentiment and overall user perceptions.
- Identify recurring customer complaints and potential friction points.
- Prioritize service improvements based on customer feedback patterns.
- Support data-driven decisions to enhance application usability and customer experience.

Ultimately, the goal is to move beyond simply collecting customer reviews toward **turning customer feedback into meaningful insights that support better business and service decisions.**
